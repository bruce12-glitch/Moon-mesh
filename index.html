For the grown-ups

Good to know.

A few things you might like to know before you open the crayon box.

  • ✦Bold lines. Easy to follow, even for small hands.
  • ✦Big shapes. Room for a whole imagination inside one page.
  • ✦No pressure. There is no wrong color. There never was.
Who are the books for?

Our big shapes and bold outlines welcome young colorers, with the collection aimed at ages 4 and up. Grown-ups are very welcome to pull up a chair, too.

How do I print the free page?

Download the PDF, open it, and print at “Fit to page” on A4 or US Letter paper. Thicker paper is lovely, but ordinary printer paper works just fine.

Where can I find Moon Mesh books?

You can find Moon Mesh on Amazon. Browse Moon Mesh on Amazon for the current selection and delivery options.

Is the online coloring studio really free?

Yes. Pick a crayon color, tap a shape, and color Miso’s page right in your browser. You can download your finished page as a PNG or print a blank copy.

What’s Moon Mail?

A little home for future studio letters and free pages. Email subscriptions are still being prepared. For now, the Coloring Club has a page ready whenever you are.
