For the grown-ups

Good to know.

Moon Mesh is a small concept studio with a big soft spot for crayons. Here’s what’s real, what’s still a sketch, and how to get coloring.

  • ✦Bold lines. Easy to follow, even for small hands.
  • ✦Big shapes. Room for a whole imagination inside one page.
  • ✦No pressure. There is no wrong color. There never was.
Who are the books for?

We’re designing them for young colorers — roughly ages 4 and up — with big shapes and bold outlines. Grown-ups are welcome to color along. We won’t tell anyone.

How do I print the free page?

Download the PDF, open it, and print at “Fit to page” on A4 or US Letter paper. Thicker paper is lovely, but ordinary printer paper works just fine.

Can I buy the books yet?

Not yet. The three titles are concept previews while the collection takes shape. The free page and the online coloring studio are ready today.

Is the online coloring studio really free?

Yes. Pick a crayon color, tap a shape, and color Miso’s page right in your browser. You can download your finished page as a PNG or print a blank copy.

What’s Moon Mail?

A monthly-ish letter with new pages, studio doodles, and the occasional moon fact. The signup on this concept site is a demo, so no email is sent yet.
