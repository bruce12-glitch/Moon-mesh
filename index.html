A Little About Us

Hello, little dreamer.

Moon Mesh is a coloring-book brand for kids who like to ask “what if?” Miso is our first little explorer. One night, the moon looked close enough to climb. So Miso grabbed a crayon and went to see.

Good to know

Who are the books for?

We’re designing them for young colorers, with big shapes and bold outlines. Grown-ups can color along, too.

How do I print the free page?

Download the PDF, open it, and print at “Fit to page” on A4 or US Letter paper.

Can I buy the books yet?
